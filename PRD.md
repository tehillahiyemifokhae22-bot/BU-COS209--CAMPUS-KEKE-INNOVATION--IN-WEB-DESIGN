## 1. Introduction

Campus Keke is a platform that is designed to make the location of available Keke around Babcock University. 

The systems goal is to make transportation within Babcock University Campus easier by providing an easy way for students to get Kekes and also the location of available Keke... instead of making the students or users wait in one position and end up running late for their classes or appointment.

This document helps explain the problem, give a suitable solution and the features this version of CampusKeke would include.


## 2. Problem Statement
It is known to all that all the peple in Babcock University (Students, Workers, Lecturers, Visitors e.t.c) use and rely on Keke's as the means and mode of transportation. The problem is the availability of this Keke's. Finding the right Keke at the right time and location can be really hard sometimes.

Students may have to trek to their desired location or end up not going anywhere because of no availabe keke. Workers/Lecturers may end up running late fpr their appointment, lecturers or other activities.

When a students wants to go from their hostel to SAT, BUCODEL, BUSA, Babrite, BUTH or any place in school... They need keke to get there and they usually wait infront of their hostels but most of the times there are no available Keke's in that area.

Keke drivers may also find it difficult locating people who need transportation. 

This is where CampusKeke comes in. The aim is to solve or lessen the length of the problem. We are offering a platform where students can videw the availability, location and status of registered keke drivers. Campus Keke will provide a system where the campus location and current availability status of registered drivers.

For example: I open the system as a user and see
Location                               Keke's Available
Hostel(e.g FAD, Emerald e.t.c)         2 available
Babrite                                4 available
Cafeteria                              3 available
BUTH                                   0 available

This can help ease students decision without having to stand in one place or walking all over the place in search for kekes.


## 3. Project Goal
The goal of CampusKeke is to make transportation within the campus easier by helping students know where available Keke's around the campus are.
## 3.2 Project Objectives
1 . Providing a platform where the availability of Keke's can be checked
2 . Helping users manage their time instead of just waiting restlessly for kekes.
3 . Improving communication between users and Keke drivers
4 . Giving keke drivers a simple way to update their availability status.
## 3.3 Expected Outcomes
Campus Keke is expected to:
-Make it easier for users to find Keke's
-Reduce the time users spend searching for keke's
-Improve access to transportation information or announcments within the university
-Help drivers connect with passengers.


## 4.1 Target Users 
1 . Users (Students, Workers, Lecturers, Visitors e.t.c)
The users use the platform to:
-View available Keke's around their location on campus
-Check if a driver is available or busy
-Pick ups at different pick up points
2 . Keke Drivers
The Keke drivers use the platform to:
-Register their details on the platform
-Update their current location on campus
-Change their availability status.
## 4.2 Stakeholders
1 . The University Transportation Managemnt: Oversee how the platform operates and ensure that registered drivers follow the transportation rues.
2 . The system Administrators: Help manage the platfrom by:
  -Approving driver registration
  -Managing users and drivers accounts
  -Suspending accounts that violate the rules of the system.

### 5.1 Purpose of the Research

The purpose of this research is to understand the transportation problems students experience when trying to find available Keke within Babcock University.

The research will also help identify the problem Keke drivers face when connecting with passengers and determine whether CampusKeke would be useful to both groups.

Questions for users
1 . How often do you use Keke within the university
2 . Wpuld you use this platform?
3 . How do you currently find Kekes?
4 . Have you ever been late to a lecture or appointment because of transportation delays?

Questions for Keke Drivers
1 . What location on campus usually have demand for kekes?
2 . How do users find you?
3 . Would you use a platform that makes it easier for users to find you?
The findings will also guide decisions about the features and requirements of the version of the platform.

## 6. Exisitng methods
Before CampusKeke, users relied on different methods to find available Keke withn Babcock, which are:
1 . Waiting infront of your hostel for minutes before finding Keke.
2 . Having to pay higher if you personal driver
3 . Having to wait outside without 100% guarrantee of getting a Keke
4 . Trekking to your desired location because you couldn't find Keke

How CampusKeke Improves the Process

CampusKeke aims to provide a centralized platform where users can view the reported availability and locations of registered Keke drivers.

Instead of depending entirely on physical searches or informal communication, users will be able to check the platform before deciding where to find transportation.

The platform will also allow drivers to update their availability status, helping users access more recent information.


## 7. Proposed Product Solution

CampusKeke will be a digital platform that allows users to view available Keke and their reported locations within Babcock University.

The platform will connect users with registered Keke drivers by providing updated availability information.

 How the Platform Will Work

1 . Keke drivers will create accounts on the platform.
2 . Administrators will review and approve drivers registrations.
3 . Approved drivers will log in and update their current campus location.
4 . Drivers will select their availability status.
5 . Users will log in or access the platform to view available Kekes.
6 . Users will be able to identify the last reported locations of available drivers.
7 . Drivers will update their status whenever they become busy or available again.

### 7.1 Expected Benefits

-Users can check availability before going to a pickup point.
-Drivers can make their availability known to potential passengers.
-Users can spend less time searching for transportation.
-Administrators can manage registered operators more effectively.


### 8.1 Features Included

The CampusKeke will include:

-User and Driver registration.
-Secure login and authentication.
-Administrator approval of Keke driver.
-Display of available Keke.
-Driver location and availability updates.

### 8.2 Features Not Included
Campus Keke will not include:

-Online payments.
-Live GPS tracking.
-Users ratings and reviews.


## 9. Functional Requirements
The system must be able to:

1 . Allow users and Keke driver to register and log in.
2 . Allow administrators to approve or reject driver accounts.
3 . Allow approved drivers to update their locations.
4 . Display available Keke drivers to users.
5 . Allow administrators to manage user accounts.


## 10. Success Criteria
CampusKeke will be successful if:

-Users can register and access the platform.
-Approved drivers can update their availability and locations.
-Users can view available Keke drivers.
-Availability information displays its last updated time.
-Administrators can successfully manage drivers and users accounts.

## 11. Conclusion
CampusKeke is proposed as the solution to transportation difficulties experienced by users within Babcock University. By providing information about available Keke and their locations, the platform aims to make campus transportation easier and faster and more convenient.
